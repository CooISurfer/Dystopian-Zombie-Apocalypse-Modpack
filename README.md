# CoolSurfer's Dystopian Zombie Apocalypse Modpack
_Forget everything you knew about survival. The dead walk, the nights are unforgiving, and the infected are everywhere, taking out the last living survivors one by one. This is no longer Minecraft. This, is the end of the world..._

  The version for this mod is Forge 1.20.1. A few mods are disabled like Iris shaders and others and are completely optional. To ensure you have the intended experience, when creating your Survival or Hardcore world, make sure you configure the Lost Cities tab. You can find it in World Options. Choose either the default experience on "test" ("kredeems_zombie_apocalypse"), or play the advanced map with a post-apocalyptic metro by clicking the "default" button once or twice.   Make sure you enable Fresh Animations and the Zombie Apocalypse provided resource packs which are essential to immersing yourself in the experience. Also you can change hotbar and lean keybinds to your liking. But using default is always great! If you enjoy this modpack pls just tell your friends about it 😁

**Tips to Have a Fun Experience:**

• Learn what the Timeless and Classics Zero mod, Immersive Engineering, Superb Warfare, [SBW] Combined Arms, and First Aid mod is. These are essential to know!

• Guns are your best friends. Without them .... let's just say "Good luck on getting far" 

• Make a fortified base as soon as possible. Sercurity Craft is your Go To for that. 🏰

• Save every living breathing villager. All it takes is two minutes for the Whole village to get wiped out. 🏘️

• Last but not least, and most important of all - MAKE THE VACCINE!!!!! 💉
